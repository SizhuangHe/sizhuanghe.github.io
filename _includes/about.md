My research interests include **generative modeling** and **multi-agent LLM systems for scientific data curation**. I have worked on discrete diffusion models that unify diffusion and autoregressive generation ([CaDDi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09767), NeurIPS 2025) and that learn distributions over finite symmetric groups ([Soft-Rank Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17353), ICML 2026). I also build LLM-based multi-agent systems for automating scientific data curation at scale.
